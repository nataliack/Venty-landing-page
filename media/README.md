# Media masters

Source files the site's assets are generated from. Nothing in here is served;
the site only ships what the tools in `tools/` produce into `public/`.

| Master | Generated by | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero/hero-master.mp4` (1920×1080, 25 fps, 636 frames) | `node tools/hero/encode.mjs` | `public/hero/sequence/` |
| `hero/archive/v1/` (first cut, 608 frames, with its encoded frames) | not used by the site | kept for reference |
| `table/table-master.mp4` (1920×1080, 24 fps, 145 frames) | `node tools/sequence/encode.mjs --name table` | `public/table/sequence/` |

## Hero video

The hero is the video played by scroll, as an image sequence: every frame of
the master, WebP, at the master's own resolution. Repeated frames (a clip
slowed down, or conformed from 24 to 25 fps, holds a frame twice) are
dropped, because a held frame reads as a stutter when scrubbed by scroll.

- `public/hero/sequence/frames/` every frame, 1920×1080
- `public/hero/sequence/manifest.json` frame count, size, a version the site
  adds to every frame URL (so a new encode is never served from cache), and
  `sourceFrames`, the master frame behind each web frame

Phones get the same frames. Which part of each frame a narrow screen shows
is the camera track (`CAMERA` in `src/components/Hero.tsx`): a focus point
per frame that glides between keys, so it can follow the action without
re-encoding anything.

Frame numbers in `Hero.tsx` (`DRESSED`, `CAMERA`) are master frames, counted
from 0 as in After Effects, so they stay right when repeats are dropped.

For a smooth scrub, export at the source clips' own frame rate (24 fps) and
without speed changes: pacing comes from the scroll, so speeding a part up
only throws frames away, and slowing it down only repeats them. The player
blends neighbouring frames by the exact scroll position, so a slow stretch
glides rather than steps; still, keep every stretch of `PACE` near the
film's own speed (the hero's end runs at about 0.4 frames per 1% of a
screen), or it reads as slow motion.

To change the video, replace `hero/hero-master.mp4` (same name) and run

```bash
node tools/hero/encode.mjs
```

Options: `--quality 92` (WebP quality), `--master <path>`. A higher-resolution
master (for example a 4K upscale) works as is: the frames are written at the
master's resolution. If the new cut changes when the dress has formed, update
`DRESSED` in `src/components/Hero.tsx` (the frame where 1.2 appears) and the
`CAMERA` keys. Before replacing it, move the current master and its frames
to `hero/archive/` if you want to keep them.

## Table film (the closing scene)

The table is played by scroll the same way (the engine is
`src/lib/sequence.ts`, shared with the hero): every frame of
`table/table-master.mp4`, WebP quality 92, at 1920×1080. It loads after the
page is up (after the loader, in idle time) or when the table comes near.
Which slice a phone sees is `CAMERA` in `src/components/closing/Closing.tsx`.
To change it, replace `table/table-master.mp4` and run

```bash
node tools/sequence/encode.mjs --name table
```

The same tool turns any `media/<name>/<name>-master.mp4` into
`public/<name>/sequence/`; add the name to the cache headers in
`next.config.ts`.

## Features

`features/ref-jersey-master.webp` (1314 × 2000, a cut-out, transparent,
cropped at the skirt) is the model in Mark up your references; the site's
copy is `public/features/ref-jersey.webp`, 880 wide, WebP 86 with its
alpha. The marks on her are drawn in the master's units (1314 × 2000), so a
new photo of another size needs them moved (`MarkUp` in
`src/components/features/Features.tsx`).

`features/turn/` holds See it on your body's turn: Jose's two clips
(`front-to-back.mp4`, `back-to-front.mp4`, 1280 × 720, 24 fps, AI video)
and `turn-master.mp4`, the two joined into one 360° turn. To change it,
replace the clips and run

```bash
node tools/turn/build.cjs media/features/turn/front-to-back.mp4 media/features/turn/back-to-front.mp4
```

It matches the second clip's colour to the first (on the frames where both
show the same pose), drops the still holds and slow ends so she turns at an
even pace with no frame shown twice, bridges the two joints with blended
frames, and writes 720 × 720 WebP frames on her, with a manifest
(`back` is the frame of her back), into `public/features/turn/`.

## Footer

The meadow in the footer is Jose's video of the field in the wind,
`footer/meadow-video-master.mp4` (1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 5 s, 121 frames).

- The loop: `footer/meadow-loop-master.mp4` (105 frames). The master's last
  frame does not lead back into its first, so its last 16 frames are
  cross-faded into its first 16 and the loop starts after them:

  ```bash
  ffmpeg -i media/footer/meadow-video-master.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v]split=3[a][b][c];[a]trim=start_frame=105:end_frame=121,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[tail];[b]trim=start_frame=0:end_frame=16,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[head];[tail][head]blend=all_expr='A*(1-(T+0.02083)/0.66667)+B*((T+0.02083)/0.66667)'[mix];[c]trim=start_frame=16:end_frame=105,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[body];[mix][body]concat=n=2:v=1[out]" -map "[out]" -an -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 10 -pix_fmt yuv420p media/footer/meadow-loop-master.mp4
  ```

- For the site, from the loop: `public/footer/meadow.mp4` (1920, H.264 CRF
  24, faststart, no sound, 1.9 MB) for desktop and `meadow-1280.mp4` (CRF 25,
  0.8 MB) for smaller screens; the first frame as `meadow-poster.webp`
  (1920) and `meadow-poster-1280.webp`.
- The mask, `public/footer/meadow-mask.png`: the hills' mist. It is the
  alpha of `footer/meadow-master.webp` (the still, 2000 × 866, transparent
  above the hills), which is the bottom of the video's frame (rows 248 to
  1080 at 1920 wide, found by matching the two), laid there on a 1920 × 1080
  frame with nothing above it, at 960 × 540. CSS sizes and places it as the
  video is (cover, from the bottom).

A new video with the same framing only needs the loop and the encodes; a
new framing needs a new mask too. `footer/mockup.webp` is Jose's mockup of
the idea.

## People

`people/natalia-chamon.webp` is the master portrait of Natalia Chamon
(1396 x 1396), framed as it should show. The announcement bar's avatar is
the whole portrait, no crop: `public/hero/natalia-chamon.webp`, saved at
60 x 60 (three times its 20px on screen), WebP quality 88. Give a new
version a new file name so browsers fetch it fresh.
